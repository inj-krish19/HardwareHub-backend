Help us test a PC troubleshooting chatbot

Takes about 10 minutes. Nothing you type here is uploaded: it stays on your device until you send the file yourself. Please do not type your name, phone number or email.

What to do

You will see short situations. For each one, type what you would write to a chatbot to get help, the way you would normally type it, short or long.

Please do not copy the situation text. Put it in your own words, as if it were your own problem. There are no right or wrong answers, and we are testing the chatbot, not you.

If a situation is not about computers, just type a question the way you would ask it.

If you were told "tester 2 of 4", enter 2 and 4. If you were not told anything, leave 1 and 1 and answer them all.
